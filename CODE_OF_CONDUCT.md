# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our
community include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes,
  and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall
  community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of
  any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards
of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in
response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive,
or harmful.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are
not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation
decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when
an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.
Examples of representing our community include using an official email address,
posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed
representative at an online or offline event.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement at
**luka@sunny-stack.com**.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the
reporter of any incident.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining
the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed
unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning from community leaders, providing
clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the
behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of
actions.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No
interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with
those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time. This
includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels
like social media. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent
ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including
sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public
communication with the community for a specified period of time. No public or
private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction
with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, is allowed during this period.
Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community
standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an
individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the
community.

## Reporting Process

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct:

### Step 1: Submit Report

**How to Report:**

- **Email:** luka@sunny-stack.com
- **Subject:** "Code of Conduct Violation Report"

**What to Include:**

- **Your Contact Information:** Name and email (for follow-up)
- **Description of Incident:** What happened, when, and where?
- **People Involved:** Who was involved? (names, usernames)
- **Witnesses:** Were there witnesses?
- **Evidence:** Screenshots, links, or other supporting evidence
- **Impact:** How has this affected you?
- **Preferred Outcome:** What resolution are you seeking?

**Confidentiality:**

- Reports are handled confidentially
- Your identity will not be disclosed without your permission
- We may need to share details with others to investigate, but only as necessary

### Step 2: Acknowledgment

**Timeline:** Within 24 hours

**What to Expect:**

- Confirmation email that we received your report
- Reference number for your report (e.g., COC-2025-001)
- Name of person handling your report
- Estimated timeline for investigation

**Example Acknowledgment:**

```
Subject: Code of Conduct Report Received - COC-2025-001

Dear [Reporter],

Thank you for reporting this incident. We take Code of Conduct violations
seriously and will investigate promptly.

Report ID: COC-2025-001
Handler: Luka D. Fagundes
Investigation Timeline: 3-5 business days

We will update you on our progress and final decision.

Best regards,
Sunny Stack Code of Conduct Team
```

### Step 3: Investigation

**Timeline:** 3-5 business days

**Process:**

1. **Review Evidence:** Examine provided screenshots, links, and context
2. **Contact Involved Parties:** Speak with reporter, subject, and witnesses
3. **Gather Additional Information:** Review community logs, Git history, communication
4. **Assess Impact:** Determine severity and community impact
5. **Consult Guidelines:** Apply Enforcement Guidelines (Correction, Warning, Temp Ban, Perm Ban)

**During Investigation:**

- You may be contacted for clarification
- We may request additional evidence
- Involved parties will be notified of the investigation
- Temporary measures may be applied (e.g., comment moderation)

### Step 4: Decision

**Timeline:** Within 7 business days of report

**Possible Outcomes:**

**No Violation Found:**

- No action taken against subject
- Explanation provided to reporter
- May provide guidance for future interactions

**Violation Confirmed:**

- Enforcement action taken per Guidelines
- Subject notified of decision and consequences
- Reporter notified of outcome (without sharing subject's personal response)

**Notification Email (to Reporter):**

```
Subject: Code of Conduct Investigation Complete - COC-2025-001

Dear [Reporter],

We have completed our investigation of your report (COC-2025-001).

Finding: [Violation Confirmed / No Violation Found]

Action Taken: [Correction / Warning / Temporary Ban / Permanent Ban]

Details: [Brief explanation without revealing personal responses]

Next Steps: [If any - e.g., continued monitoring]

If you have questions or concerns about this decision, you may appeal within
7 days by replying to this email.

Thank you for helping maintain a respectful community.

Best regards,
Sunny Stack Code of Conduct Team
```

**Notification Email (to Subject):**

```
Subject: Code of Conduct Violation - Action Required

Dear [Subject],

A Code of Conduct violation report has been filed regarding your behavior in
[location/context]. After investigation, we have determined that your actions
violated our community standards.

Violation: [Specific behavior that violated Code of Conduct]

Evidence: [Brief summary - links to comments, commits, etc.]

Action: [Correction / Warning / Temporary Ban / Permanent Ban]

Effective: [Immediately / Specified date]

Expectations:
- [Specific behavioral expectations]
- [Restrictions during ban period, if applicable]
- [Requirements for reinstatement, if applicable]

Appeal Process:
You may appeal this decision within 7 days by replying to this email with:
- Your perspective on the incident
- Any mitigating circumstances
- Commitment to behavioral change

We expect all community members to uphold our Code of Conduct. Future violations
may result in more severe consequences, including permanent ban.

Best regards,
Sunny Stack Code of Conduct Team
```

### Step 5: Communication

**Timeline:** Immediately after decision

**To Reporter:**

- Outcome of investigation
- Action taken (general terms)
- Option to appeal

**To Subject:**

- Specific violation details
- Consequences and expectations
- Appeal process

**To Community (if applicable):**

- For serious violations (Temp Ban, Perm Ban), we may publish a community notice:
  - General description (no personal details)
  - Action taken
  - Reaffirmation of community standards

### Step 6: Appeal (Optional)

**Timeline:** 7 days to submit appeal

**How to Appeal:**

- Reply to decision email with subject "Appeal: COC-[Report ID]"
- Include:
  - Your perspective on the incident
  - Any evidence not previously considered
  - Mitigating circumstances
  - Commitment to behavioral change

**Appeal Review:**

- Reviewed by original handler + independent reviewer
- Timeline: 5 business days
- Decision is final (no further appeals)

**Possible Outcomes:**

- **Appeal Granted:** Original decision reversed or reduced
- **Appeal Denied:** Original decision stands
- **Appeal Partially Granted:** Modified consequences

## Examples of Enforcement

### Example 1: Correction (Minor)

**Incident:** Contributor uses insensitive language in pull request comment

**Report:** Another contributor reports feeling uncomfortable

**Investigation:** Review PR comments, contact both parties

**Decision:** Correction

**Action:**

- Private message to contributor explaining why language was inappropriate
- Request for public apology in PR thread
- Link to Code of Conduct
- No further action if behavior improves

**Outcome:** Contributor apologizes, edits comment, no recurrence

---

### Example 2: Warning (Moderate)

**Incident:** Contributor repeatedly dismisses feedback from junior contributors

**Report:** Multiple reports from different contributors

**Investigation:** Review comment history, speak with involved parties

**Decision:** Warning

**Action:**

- Private written warning explaining pattern of behavior
- 30-day no-contact period with reporters
- Required to refrain from reviewing PRs from junior contributors (30 days)
- Future violations may result in temporary ban

**Outcome:** Contributor acknowledges pattern, improves behavior

---

### Example 3: Temporary Ban (Serious)

**Incident:** Contributor sends harassing direct messages to another contributor

**Report:** Screenshots of DMs provided

**Investigation:** Verify authenticity of screenshots, contact both parties

**Decision:** Temporary Ban (90 days)

**Action:**

- Banned from GitHub repository (issues, PRs, comments)
- Banned from Discord server (if applicable)
- No communication with community members
- Can reapply after 90 days with written statement of understanding

**Outcome:** Ban enforced, no contact during period, review before reinstatement

---

### Example 4: Permanent Ban (Severe)

**Incident:** Contributor doxes another contributor (publishes private information)

**Report:** Link to public post with personal information

**Investigation:** Verify information posted, assess impact

**Decision:** Permanent Ban

**Action:**

- Immediate permanent ban from all community spaces
- Reported to platform (GitHub, Discord) for ToS violations
- May report to authorities if applicable
- Public notice to community warning of doxing incident

**Outcome:** Banned contributor cannot return, community informed to protect others

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What if I'm unsure if something is a violation?

**Answer:** When in doubt, report it. We'd rather review a borderline case than miss a serious violation. You can also email luka@sunny-stack.com to discuss anonymously before filing a formal report.

### Will my report be kept confidential?

**Answer:** Yes, we treat all reports confidentially. We will not share your identity without your explicit permission, except when required by law.

### What if the person I'm reporting is a maintainer?

**Answer:** Report to luka@sunny-stack.com. If the maintainer is the handler, an independent third party will review the case to ensure fairness.

### Can I report anonymously?

**Answer:** Yes, you can send an anonymous report to luka@sunny-stack.com. However, we may not be able to follow up with you for additional information, which could limit our investigation.

### What happens if I'm falsely accused?

**Answer:** We investigate all reports thoroughly. False reports are rare, but if we determine a report was made in bad faith, the reporter may face consequences under this Code of Conduct.

### Can I be banned for actions outside the community?

**Answer:** Generally, no. We only enforce violations in community spaces or when representing the project. However, serious criminal behavior (threats, doxing, harassment) may result in bans to protect the community.

### How long do bans last?

**Answer:** Depends on severity:

- **Temporary Ban:** 30-90 days (may be longer for serious violations)
- **Permanent Ban:** Indefinite, may be appealed after 1 year in exceptional circumstances

### What if I disagree with the enforcement decision?

**Answer:** You can appeal within 7 days by replying to the decision email. Include your perspective, any new evidence, and demonstrate commitment to behavioral change.
